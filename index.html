SAFETY

BUILD SMART.

VERIFY

Check identities, businesses, references and agreements independently.

PROTECT INFORMATION

Do not share passwords, banking credentials or sensitive documents with unknown people.

MEET SAFELY

For first meetings, use appropriate public or professional environments and tell someone where you are.

DON'T SEND MONEY BLINDLY

Use written agreements and verify payment details before transferring funds.

REPORT

Keep evidence and report suspicious behaviour through the relevant channels.

IMPORTANT
