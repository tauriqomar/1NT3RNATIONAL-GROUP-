SAFETY

NETWORK SMART.

VERIFY PEOPLE

Independently check identities, businesses and references before relying on anyone.

PROTECT INFORMATION

Do not share passwords, banking credentials or sensitive documents with unknown people.

AGREE CLEARLY

Put important business, payment and project terms in writing.

MEET SAFELY

Use sensible precautions when meeting someone for the first time.

REPORT PROBLEMS

Keep evidence and report suspicious or abusive behaviour through appropriate channels.
